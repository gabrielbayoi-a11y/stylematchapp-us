StyleMatch GA

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StyleMatch GA collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when salons and stylists use our services.

Effective date: September 3, 2026
Website: https://stylematchapp.us
Privacy contact: support@stylematchapp.us
StyleMatch GA connects salons and professional stylists in Georgia. This policy applies to the StyleMatch GA mobile app, website, and related services.

1. Who We Are

StyleMatch GA is a marketplace and workforce coordination platform that helps salons discover, request, and book stylists, and helps stylists discover and accept salon work opportunities.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

  • Name, email address, phone number, and login credentials.
  • Role selection, business name, experience, professional profile information, and specialties.
  • Profile photos, portfolio photos, salon photos, and related content.
  • City, state, ZIP code, salon address, and service-area information.

Jobs, applications, and bookings

  • Job posts, mission type, schedule, time slot, location, description, and requirements.
  • Applications, acceptance/rejection decisions, bookings, cancellations, no-shows, check-ins, and shift status.
  • Early shift ending reasons, emergency replacement activity, and shift audit events.

Location information

We may collect precise or approximate location data for nearby job discovery, salon location validation, check-in verification, distance calculations, and emergency replacement matching.

We do not sell precise location data.

Photos and Proof of Work

Stylists may upload Front, Side, and Back Proof of Work photos. Staffing shifts may include multiple proof sets for multiple completed services. Proof of Work is attached to the booking and is not automatically added to a public portfolio.

Payment and Labor Pay information

We may collect payment method preference, Labor Pay totals, detailed Labor Pay line items, specialty/service categories, payment-request status, and payment-confirmation status.

Third-party providers may process subscriptions or future marketplace payments. We do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers when handled by an external processor.

Support, reports, and reliability

  • Support messages, reports, dispute information, and issue descriptions.
  • Ratings, reviews, reliability events, and operational audit history.
  • Notification history and related event information.

Technical information

We may collect device type, operating system, app version, IP address, timestamps, diagnostic information, and technical logs for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage Salon and Stylist accounts.
  • Match stylists with salon opportunities.
  • Process applications, bookings, check-ins, shift activity, and emergency replacement workflows.
  • Support Proof of Work, Labor Pay, payment requests, and payment confirmations.
  • Send account, booking, application, payment, security, and operational notifications.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, misuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain audit, reliability, support, and dispute records.
  • Provide support and respond to privacy or account requests.
  • Improve app performance, usability, security, and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Notifications

StyleMatch GA uses notifications for applications, bookings, shift changes, Proof of Work, payment requests, emergency replacements, account activity, and other relevant updates.

Opening a notification may mark it as read. Notifications may remain in the in-app notification history until the user explicitly clears them or they are removed under our retention practices.

5. How We Share Information

  • Other StyleMatch users: information needed to evaluate profiles, jobs, applications, bookings, Proof of Work, Labor Pay requests, or shift actions.
  • Service providers: hosting, database, email, notifications, maps/location, storage, security, analytics, and payment providers.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business successors: in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Third-Party Services

StyleMatch GA may use third-party providers for cloud hosting, databases, email delivery, push notifications, maps/geocoding, app distribution, subscription billing, and payment processing. These providers process information under their own terms and contractual obligations.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access, encrypted network communications where supported, access controls, secure credential handling, and operational logging.

No transmission or storage method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention

We retain information as long as reasonably necessary to provide StyleMatch GA, maintain business and audit records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Some booking, payment, support, audit, reliability, and dispute records may be retained after account closure where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, compliance, or dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request account deletion through StyleMatch GA's account-deletion functionality or by contacting support@stylematchapp.us.

A public account-deletion request page is intended to be available at https://stylematchapp.us/delete-account.

When an account is deleted, information no longer required is deleted or de-identified, subject to retention needed for legal, payment, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute, or security purposes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or additional information about personal data associated with your account.

To submit a privacy request, contact support@stylematchapp.us. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

11. Children's Privacy

StyleMatch GA is intended for professional salon and stylist use and is not directed to children. Users must meet applicable age requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. U.S. State Privacy Rights

Users in certain U.S. states may have additional rights under state privacy laws. Where applicable, StyleMatch GA will respond to valid requests consistent with those laws.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as StyleMatch GA evolves. Material changes may be communicated through the app, website, or other appropriate means. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
