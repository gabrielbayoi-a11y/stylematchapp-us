Delete Your StyleMatch GA Account

Request deletion of your Salon or Stylist account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@stylematchapp.us from the email address linked to your StyleMatch GA account.
  2. Use the subject StyleMatch GA Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your account email address and whether the account is a Salon or Stylist account.
  4. We may verify account ownership before completing the request.

Where available, you may also use the account deletion option inside the StyleMatch GA app.

Data deleted or de-identified

  • Profile information and contact details.
  • Profile and portfolio photos not required for retained records.
  • Account preferences and settings.
  • Other personal data not required for legal, security, payment, audit, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes.

Data that may be retained

  • Completed booking and transaction records.
  • Payment request and payment confirmation records.
  • Support, dispute, report, reliability, and audit records.
  • Records required by law or needed to prevent abuse or fraud.

Where possible, retained records will be minimized or de-identified.
